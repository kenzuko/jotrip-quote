# Bespoke personal-data notice - pending approval

Before enabling `/bespoke/api/submit`, JoTrip must review and publish a privacy notice in the correct languages. At minimum identify the operator and a working contact email, the specific purpose of the form, categories of personal information submitted, access by JoTrip staff and any service providers, storage and deletion period, the individual's options to correct/delete data, and where to make a request. Confirm any applicable notices/consents with qualified counsel for the guest markets served.

This document is **not** the privacy notice and not a legal-compliance determination. Until the final notice is reviewed, the Worker refuses submissions via `PRIVACY_NOTICE_APPROVED=false`. Never set that flag to `true` just to make testing pass on a public preview.
